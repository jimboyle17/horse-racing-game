How to Play

🏷️

1. Buy at Auction

Visit Tattersalls to browse horses. Set your maximum bid and compete against AI trainers.

🐴

2. Train Your Stable

Each horse has unique attributes. Focus training on speed, stamina, acceleration, or temperament.

🏁

3. Race to Win

Choose your race strategy, then make key decisions during the race. Your horse's attributes affect outcomes.

🏆

4. Become Champion

Earn points from race placements. End the season as Champion Trainer to prove your mastery.
